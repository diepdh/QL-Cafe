# Goal

Sua 3 van de chuc nang con ton trong app `QuanLyCF` ma khong lam vo nghiep vu hien tai:

1. Man hinh Nha cung cap dang hien nut `+ Them NCC` nhung bam khong co tac dung.
2. Phan danh muc hang hoa chua co chuc nang them danh muc moi tu UI quan ly san pham.
3. Phan cong thuc pha che hien chi ho tro NVL tinh che, trong khi nghiep vu thuc te can dung duoc ca NVL tho va NVL tinh che trong cung mot cong thuc.

Ket qua mong muon:

- Coder co task nho, scope ro, de review.
- Hai van de dau duoc sua nhu bugfix/CRUD thong thuong.
- Van de cong thuc duoc tach rieng thanh task co y thuc ve schema/API, tranh lam am scope.

Rang buoc:

- Khong gop ca 3 van de vao mot task.
- Khong sua backend `.gs` ngoai pham vi can thiet.
- Neu task cong thuc can doi schema `RECIPES`, phai ghi ro migration/compatibility.
